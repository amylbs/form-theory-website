About me

Hi, I'm Amy. I grew up in the Colorado mountains playing team sports, skiing, and living outdoors. Later in my adult life I found solo endurance sports. I enjoyed running, open-water swimming, and eventually competing in triathlons.

Over time my activity and training has shifted to strength and functional mobility. This transition that started over 5 years ago has inspired me to work in the field to support others in their own fitness and health journeys with intentional and tailored bodywork in Berlin, Germany.

What I offer
